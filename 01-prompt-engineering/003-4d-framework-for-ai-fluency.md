## The Problem

You're using AI tools regularly but getting inconsistent or ethically murky results — because you lack a structured way to think about human-AI collaboration.

## What I Learned

The 4D Framework (Prof. Rick Dakan & Prof. Joseph Feller, partnered with Anthropic) defines AI fluency as working with AI effectively, efficiently, ethically, and safely through four competencies:

- Delegation — Decide what to give AI vs. keep for yourself. Understand AI capabilities and choose the right mode: Automation (AI executes), Augmentation (you collaborate), or Agency (AI acts independently on your behalf).

- Description — Communicate clearly with AI. Define outputs, provide context, specify behaviors, and craft prompts that guide AI toward useful results.

- Discernment — Evaluate outputs critically. Assess quality, accuracy, and appropriateness — then iterate. The Description–Discernment loop is the core engine of productive AI work.

- Diligence — Act responsibly. Be transparent about AI's role, choose systems thoughtfully, and take full accountability for AI-assisted outputs you publish or share.

## Example

```markdown
Task: Draft a market research summary using AI

Delegation → Assign data synthesis to AI; keep strategic interpretation yourself
Description → Prompt: "Summarize these 5 reports into key trends for a B2B SaaS audience,
focusing on pricing and churn signals. Bullet points, max 300 words."
Discernment → Review output: accurate? nuanced? Revise prompt and re-run as needed.
Diligence → Disclose AI assistance in the doc; verify all statistics before sharing.
```

## Why It Matters

The 4D Framework is tool-agnostic — it keeps your AI practice structured and responsible regardless of which model or platform you use next.
